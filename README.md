# Feng Wang

**Professor, Changzhou University**  
**Statistical Machine Learning | Statistical Modeling | Mathematical Modeling**

I work at the intersection of statistical machine learning and applied mathematics, with a foundation in differential equations and dynamical systems.

## Research Interests

- Statistical learning, including deep forest methods, generalized additive models, and granular computing.
- Statistical modeling and data analysis.
- Differential equations, nonlinear dynamics, and stability of periodic solutions.

## Mathematical Background

- **Ph.D., Hohai University**, 2016.
- **Postdoctoral researcher, Department of Mathematics, Nanjing University**, 2016-2019.
- Mathematical research published in journals including *Mathematische Annalen* and the *Journal of Differential Equations*.

## Mathematical Modeling and Teaching

I teach mathematical modeling, probability and mathematical statistics, linear algebra, and calculus. Student teams I have supervised have earned seven national first prizes and 21 national second prizes in mathematical modeling competitions.
